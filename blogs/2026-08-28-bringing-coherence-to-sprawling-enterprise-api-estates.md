I am working hard to make sense of the programmability of the companies, organizations, institutions, and government agencies I profile at scale, and that work gets expressed through my APIs.io search engine. One new dimension I added with last night’s build is the concept of an estate . I needed something that would help tame the sprawl of large enterprises and the work they have done across the years, across lines of business, across teams, and across acquisitions.
