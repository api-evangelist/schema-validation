While building a signup form for my new CentralPark.Guide website I was using Claude to help me deploy a Cloudflare worker to handle the submission of form and the emails that get sent to me and to end-users. As part of the setup Claude asked me to clarify one thing that I thought was worth tuning into: Email provider: I’ll write the Worker for Resend (simplest API, free tier, just an API key). If you’d rather use AWS SES, Mailgun, or Postmark, say so. You can see the future of product placement and advertising here. Advertisers and AI providers have a lot of data.
